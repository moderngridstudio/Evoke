Quick look

Controls how the Quick Look button and modal look everywhere they appear. Turn Quick Look on or off per section (Collection page, Search, Featured collection tabs, Product recommendations, Shop the look, Collage).

Size guide

Adds a size guide link beside the matching option inside the Quick Look modal. Pick the page holding your size chart — its content opens in a panel over the modal, so the shopper never leaves the collection.
